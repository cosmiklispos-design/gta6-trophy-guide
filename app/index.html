Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission replay — but free-roam continues after the story, and only one trophy is possibly missable. Keep cheats off: they disable trophies and saving.

What 100% completion actually wants

The checklist opens after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion — most side quests are not needed, and partial rows only ask for 70–80% of their totals.

Save smart. There is no mission replay and 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup.

Missable trophies

Good news: only one trophy is possibly missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam.

Everything else is safe. Optional missions that disappear, quest givers who go quiet for a chapter, fences that close and magazine collectibles in story buildings — none of it blocks a trophy or 100% completion, because the game offers far more than the checklist needs (for example 200–300+ optional missions against the 100 required).

Sources & notes

Every guide in this app is written in our own words. The facts behind each one are cited beside it, from the sources below. Official trophy names and descriptions are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.

    What we could not verify

    • Strangers & Freaks: only some stranger strands count toward the 8, and guides are still confirming the full set. The strands named in that trophy's guide are the confirmed ones (PowerPyx).
    • Positive Mental Attitude: it is still unclear whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is required for the trophy. Do it anyway — it costs a minute and the guide who earned the trophy had done it (PowerPyx).
    • The Truth Is Out There: the UFO spawn is slightly random and not guaranteed on a given night (PowerPyx).
    • Fair Game: exactly which animal pops the trophy is still being confirmed (PowerPyx).
    • Rockstar has not published an official trophy list of its own; the list here is the one Exophase and PSNProfiles carry, and the guides that agree with it.
